Project 03 2024 Live TV Production

Western-
ized

How the European youth is influenced by cowboy media — a cross-cultural live TV production filmed in Flagstaff, Arizona.

01 — Concept

Cowboy culture meets Gen Z.

'Westernized' introduces audiences to a vibrant, modern perspective on cowboy culture. With a fresh take that highlights everything from fashion trends to rodeo scenes and the art of Western life, the show aimed to connect with Generation Z by showcasing cowboy traditions in ways that resonate with their lifestyles and interests.

Each segment — from cowboy fashion to social life and art — offered viewers a deeper understanding of a cultural phenomenon that blends tradition with contemporary appeal. The show asked: why are European youth so drawn to this aesthetic, and what does it say about who we are?

02 — Research

From the Netherlands to Arizona.

The research phase started in the Netherlands, with the team generating ideas and conducting desk research to find the niche they wanted to target. From there, research became hands-on — reaching out directly to protagonists for each segment of the show.

The finished product was executed in Flagstaff, Arizona, USA, making this a genuinely cross-cultural experience that shaped both the content and the team behind it.

03 — Segment Production

Jose and the art of the hat.

As segment producer, I had hands-on experience designing the look and feel of my segment alongside the director. We chose to dive into Jose's love for hat-making — a craft rooted in generations of cowboy heritage in his family — and the passion that drives him to keep that tradition alive.

The segment was built around a person, not a spectacle. That made the difference.

04 — Development

From Breda to the American West.

Pre-production started in Breda, Netherlands, with the team generating ideas and desk research. Once on the ground in the States, the real work began. From developing graphics to building the set, the team collaborated across every step of the production process to deliver the best show they could.

This was live TV production in the truest sense — no second takes on location, no safety net. Just preparation, adaptability, and a team that trusted each other under pressure.
