Pijn-
stad

Your digital haven for understanding, connecting, and managing chronic pain — designed in collaboration with the Landelijke Pijn Organisatie.

01 — Concept

A space for people in pain.

The Pijnstad App was designed in collaboration with the Landelijke Pijn Organisatie (LPO), a Dutch national pain organisation. The concept: create a digital space that caters to everyone dealing with chronic pain — not just as a health tool, but as a genuine community and support platform.

Chronic pain is isolating. Pijnstad aimed to change that by building a product that connected users to resources, to each other, and to the tools they need to manage their daily lives.

02 — Research

Understanding before designing.

To develop the Pijnstad App, the team conducted thorough research to understand the specific needs of individuals with chronic pain. This involved reviewing academic literature on chronic pain, conducting in-depth user interviews, analysing user needs in context, and studying existing health and wellness apps to understand what was missing.

This research phase provided the foundation for every design decision — ensuring the app would meet the unique, often overlooked requirements of its target audience rather than defaulting to generic health-app conventions.

03 — Design

Blues for reliability and calm.

The design incorporated the LPO's primary colour palette of blues — chosen to represent reliability and serenity for users managing a condition that is often unpredictable and stressful. Secondary and accent colours were applied strategically to guide the user experience without overwhelming it.

Typography, navigation patterns, and interaction models were all documented in Figma, ensuring the design system was reproducible and extensible for future development phases.

04 — Development

Built for the people who need it most.

The Pijnstad App offers community forums, private chats, informational resources, pain tracking tools, and personalised reminders. Every feature decision traced back to something a real user said they needed.

The app prioritised user-friendliness, security, and accessibility across iOS and Android platforms — recognising that chronic pain affects people regardless of age, technical ability, or device preference.
